Cover Drive turns your photo and a caption into GTA 6-style poster cover art: multi-panel layouts (Triptych, Split, Hero + strip), per-panel crop and positioning by drag, zoom and pan, bold Anton poster lettering with an editable title and subtitle, three original colour grades (Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Golden Hour), and one-click PNG export in 1080x1080, 1080x1350 and 1920x1080. Everything runs offline in the browser — photos are read on your machine and never uploaded — and the title font (Anton, SIL Open Font License) ships under app/fonts/. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6 Cover Art” bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — upload a photo (or use a built-in sample), pick a layout, write your caption, choose a grade, and hit Export PNG.
